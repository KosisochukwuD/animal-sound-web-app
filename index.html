Learn with Animal Soundss

Have fun learning animal sounds

Duck – A water bird with webbed feet. It makes a quack.

Elephant – The largest land animal. It makes a trumpet.

Dog – A loyal domestic animal. It makes a bark.

Cricket – An insect known for its chirping sound. It makes a chirp.

Snake – A legless reptile. It makes a hiss.

Cat – A small domestic pet. It makes a meow.

Cow – A farm animal that gives milk. It makes a moo.

Horse – A strong animal used for riding. It makes a neigh.

Goose – A water bird larger than a duck. It makes a honk.

Rooster – A male chicken. It makes a crow ("cock-a-doodle-doo").

Goat – A farm animal that gives milk. It makes a bleat.

Lion – The king of the jungle. It makes a roar.
